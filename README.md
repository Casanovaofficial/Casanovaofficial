# Hey, I'm Erick

I'm a software developer and AI automation consultant based in Tijuana. I didn't come from a CS degree. I came from sales, cold calling and living inside HubSpot all day. I started building small tools to make that job easier, the tools got bigger, and now building them is the job.

Today I lead AI and IT for two clinics and a U.S. real estate acquisitions team, and I take freelance automation work on the side. Most of what I build sits between a lead and a sale: chatbots that qualify people on WhatsApp, Instagram or a website, CRM automations so nobody forgets to follow up, and the apps and dashboards managers check every week.

## Where I'm working now

### Cellular Institute, Cancún
Head of AI & IT for a regenerative medicine clinic. I built the system that takes a patient from first message to the medical team. A bilingual chatbot on the website, WhatsApp and Instagram asks the pre-qualification questions, scores each lead Hot, Warm or Cold on six criteria, and hands it to HubSpot, where follow-up agents and an executive dashboard take over.

I also wrote the clinic's internal app (Vue 3 and TypeScript on the front, Node/Express and MongoDB behind it, shipped with Docker and GitHub Actions) and connected QuickBooks, PandaDoc, Google Drive and Sheets in Make so quotes and contracts stop getting copied by hand.

### Nadax & TruBuyers
Head of AI & IT for a U.S. real estate acquisitions team buying from distressed sellers in Georgia, Florida and Maryland. I started there as a cold caller and moved up to Lead Manager before taking over AI and IT.

Each acquisition rep has a Custom GPT connected to HubSpot through its API, so they can update deals, review missed opportunities and pull their task list by asking in plain language. I write and tune the prompts for our SMS bot and the AI receptionist on Aloware, built a Chrome extension that moves applicants from Indeed into HubSpot, and built the KPI dashboards management reviews every week.

### Immunotherapy Wellness Center
Head of Automations & AI. I audited the clinic's website, built a bilingual chatbot in native code, and I'm connecting the site, HubSpot and Make so every form turns into a tracked lead with follow-up and scheduling.

### Tortuga Nest
Freelance. I set up their CRM automations and AI lead pre-qualification using the same model that works at Nadax, adapted to their team.

### New City Business Owners Club, Tijuana
Freelance, for a business owners' association in the Diamond Tower. I set up the club's intake so inquiries from the website, WhatsApp and events land in one CRM with automatic follow-up, and I build custom tools for members' own businesses.

### Nexus Media
My freelance automation work for agencies and service businesses. For Phoenix AI Marketing I built a full GoHighLevel funnel, from paid ads to a booked call, with 6 workflows, 21 tags and an 8-stage pipeline, then moved it off WhatsApp after Meta flagged the bot traffic. For Silver Line Climate and LV Windows I set up GoHighLevel with round-robin lead routing and TCPA-compliant texting. I'm also building out operations automation for The Wolf Legal Services in Arizona, and I run a Make pipeline that writes, illustrates and publishes WordPress posts.
